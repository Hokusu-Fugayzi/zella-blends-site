Meet the Apothecary

Our Story

Where heaven meets the earth — handcrafted for beauty, for wellness, and for the care of body, soul, and spirit.

Zella Blends began with a personal search — for healing, and for a way to hold together the things Tab loves most: the old wisdom of stones, the oils, and the quiet work of setting an intention and meaning it.

What started as a private practice at her own table became something larger: a calling to share it. Every Zella Blend still carries that first intention — to make something beautiful and useful, in a way that honors both the earth and the Divine.

“I believe wellness isn't something you buy — it's something you practice. These blends are invitations to slow down, to pay attention, and to remember how much of the good was given to us already.” Tab, Founder & Apothecary

The Practice

Every bottle is composed by hand in small batch — never mass-produced. Stones are chosen for what they have long been carried for; oils for the way they meet the body and the breath. Nothing leaves the table without an intention set into it, and a blessing over it.

Where Heaven Meets the Earth

Zella lives in the tender overlap — prayer and ritual, Creation and mystery, reverence and a little Halloween-hearted magic. The work is grounded but never without wonder: something quiet you can carry on your wrists, your temples, the still center of your chest.
