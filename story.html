Meet the Alchemist

Our Story

Where alchemy meets the earth — handcrafted at the intersection of health, beauty, and ancient wisdom.

Zella Blends was born from Tab's deeply personal journey — a search for healing that led through ancient crystal wisdom, sacred oils, and the transformative power of intention-setting.

What started as a private practice of blending oils with healing stones became something bigger: a calling to share this alchemy with others. Every Zella Blend carries the energy of that original intention — to create tools for transformation that honor both the earth and the spirit.

“I believe that wellness isn't something you buy — it's something you practice. These blends are invitations to slow down, connect inward, and remember the magic that lives within you.” Tab, Founder & Alchemist

The Practice

Every bottle is composed by hand in small batch — never mass-produced. Stones are chosen for their vibrational properties; oils for the way they meet the body and the breath. Nothing leaves the table without an intention set into it.
