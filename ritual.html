The Practice

The Ritual

A short, daily moment to anoint the wrists, the temples, the heart — and to let intention settle alongside scent. Less than a minute, for body, soul, and spirit.

A Zella Blend is not a fragrance you apply and forget. It is an invitation to pause. The ritual takes less than a minute, and it asks only that you mean it.

01

Still

Hold the bottle in both hands. Take one slow breath in, and a slower one out. Let the day fall away from your shoulders before you begin.

02

Set

Name your intention — a word, a feeling, a quiet hope. Call it prayer or intention, whichever word is yours. Say it once, silently; the blend was made with care already in it, and this is you meeting it halfway.

03

Anoint

Roll the oil onto the pulse points: inner wrists, behind the ears, the base of the throat, the still center of the chest. Move without hurry.

04

Breathe

Bring your wrists to your face. Inhale three times — long, full, unhurried — and let the scent carry the intention inward.

05

Return

Through the day, when you catch the scent again, let it call you back to the intention you set. That is the whole of it.

“Wellness isn't something you buy — it's something you practice.” Tab, Founder & Apothecary

When to wear it

A morning blend opens the day. An evening blend softens its edges. Some keep one at the altar for meditation and journaling; others carry it and return to it often. There is no wrong moment — only the one you choose.
