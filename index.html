Meet the Alchemist

Meet Tab --
The Alchemist Behind Zella

Zella Blends was born from Tab's deeply personal journey -- a search for healing that led through ancient crystal wisdom, sacred oils, and the transformative power of intention-setting.

What started as Tab's private practice of blending oils with healing stones became something bigger: a calling to share this alchemy with others. Every Zella Blend carries the energy of that original intention -- to create tools for transformation that honor both the earth and the spirit.

"I believe that wellness isn't something you buy -- it's something you practice. These blends are invitations to slow down, connect inward, and remember the magic that lives within you."
-- Tab, Founder & Alchemist
