Why let someone else choose

Most of us reach for the same scent over and over — the one that already feels like us. There's nothing wrong with that. But a year of only what you'd have picked is a smaller year than it could be.

The Circle is a way of being handed something. Some months it will be the blend you'd have chosen anyway. Some months it will be the one you'd have walked past — and that's usually the one that ends up on the nightstand.

Questions

Before you join

Can I really cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no contract and no minimum. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again — the bundle already on its way is still yours.

What if I already own one of the blends?

Tell us what's already on your shelf and we'll work around it. A second bottle of something you love is never wasted, but the point is to widen the collection, not repeat it.

Can I say what I don't like?

Please do. If a note doesn't agree with you — florals, smoke, anything — say so when you join and it stays out of your box. Everything else is left to the apothecary.

Is it a gift-able thing?

It's one of the better ones. Tell us how many months and we'll send it with a note in your handwriting rather than a receipt.

When does it ship?

Bundles go out in the first week of each month. Join at any point and your first box goes with the next run.
